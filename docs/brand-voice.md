## Writing approach

Use short, concrete sentences and explain what a visitor can do next. Build the
founder's story from his own answers. Public learning outcomes may be broad;
they must not reveal the teaching sequence or signature methods.

Avoid invented quotations, generic slogans, unconfirmed credentials or schedules,
promises of mastery, and negative judgements about other congregations. Online
delivery should describe how students reach the teacher, not suggest that the
Academy is modernizing the church's tradition.
